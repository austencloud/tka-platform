What Is TKA

TKA is a notation system for flow arts. It does for staff spinning, fan manipulation, and club juggling what sheet music does for musicians: write it down, read it back, reproduce it later.

Each beat of movement gets a pictograph. The pictograph shows where both hands are on a grid, how they move, and in which direction. Positions are readable at a glance: hands across from each other, at the same point, or at a right angle. String the pictographs together and you have a sequence.

The system was built for double staves but works with fans, clubs, hoops, and buugeng. Props you grip directly and wield in both hands.

Because TKA uses symbols instead of English terminology, it works across languages. A pictograph means the same thing whether you learned to spin in Portland or Seoul. Patterns get written down, shared, and built upon. Choreographers can collaborate without needing a shared spoken vocabulary.
