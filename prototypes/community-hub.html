How TKA works

Positions

Hands sit at points on a diamond grid. Where they are is the position.

Letters

Moving from one position to another is a letter. The path, timing, and direction are all encoded.

Sequences

String letters together and you get a sequence. Each one is readable, repeatable, and shareable.
