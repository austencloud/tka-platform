Fitting text into the decomposed equation layout

Three ways to reclaim space for the LOOP explanation while keeping blue + red = combined.

A: Compact Equation Row

Shrink the blue + red to a small row near the top. Combined mandala gets the center. Text fits below. Reads top-down: word, equation, result, explanation.

B: Hero + Mini Equation

Combined mandala stays large and central (like Option 2 from before). The blue + red decomposition is tiny at the bottom alongside the text. Best of both: dramatic center, educational footnote.

C: Tight Vertical Stack

Everything stacks: word, small blue + red row, combined mandala, then 2-3 lines of text. Compact but readable. The combined mandala is slightly smaller than Option B to make room.
