How does the mandala live on the card back?

Three ways to integrate the mandala into V5. All keep the corner badges (level, LOOP, steps, start position).

A: Watermark Background

Mandala rendered at low opacity behind all existing V5 content. Word and text stay prominent. The mandala adds texture and uniqueness without competing for space. Elegant, subtle.

B: Mandala Hero

Word at top, mandala takes the center stage at full opacity, LOOP explanation below. The mandala IS the focal point of the card back. Bold, visual-first.

C: Decomposed Equation

Word at top, then blue mandala + red mandala = combined mandala. Educational: shows how each hand contributes to the whole. Teaches the VTG layering concept visually.

You can also combine these: e.g. decomposed equation on one view, watermark on another. Click the one that excites you most for the card back.
