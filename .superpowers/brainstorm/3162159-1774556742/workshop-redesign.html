What Changed

Card Shape

Wide rectangular cards with text walls
Tall portrait cards with abstract hero area
Each workshop feels like a poster, not a form row.

Action Buttons

Copy / Edit / Delete on every card
Removed entirely from card surface
Click card to open modal. Edit/delete live there. The card is for showing, not managing.

Level Indicator

Loud colored banner across full header
3px accent line + small dot + label at bottom
Color is present but not screaming.

Props Display

Text chips: "double-staves", "clubs"
Small circular icons in the hero area
Visual personality without text clutter.

Description

3-line truncated paragraph with "Show more"
Single line teaser, full details in modal
Cards breathe. Content lives inside.

Add Workshop

Dashed border empty card in the grid
Single button in the header area
Doesn't break the showcase illusion.

Hero Area

Abstract geometric patterns (circles + lines) tinted by level color
Gives each card visual weight without requiring photos. Patterns are decorative, not data.

Interaction Model

Click anywhere on card → opens detail/edit modal
Hover lifts card with shadow. Entire card is the click target. No buttons competing for attention.
