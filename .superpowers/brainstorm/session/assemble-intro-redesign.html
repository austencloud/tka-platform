Big, centered instruction — "Step 1 of 2: Tap a starting point" replaces the tucked-away "Tap start for blue." User knows exactly what to do and where they are in the process.
2
Warm card background — subtle gradient replaces flat black. Rounded corners (20px), soft inner glow, and a faint border give it depth without distraction.
3
Bigger hit targets — diamond grid points are 52px diameter (vs ~40px current), with a glowing pulse that says "tap me." Way easier on mobile.
4
Bubbly turn bar — pills are taller (44px min-height), wider, rounded (10px). The whole bar has a card treatment with its own gradient background. Feels like part of the UI, not floating glass.
5
Open question: Should the non-diamond points (the white intercardinal dots at N/S/E/W) still show during idle? They're not tappable targets in diamond mode — hiding them reduces noise.
