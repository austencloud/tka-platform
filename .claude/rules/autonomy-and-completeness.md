# Autonomy and Completion Contract

- Resolve observable facts with repository inspection and available tools.
- Ask only for a material product choice, user-only credential or interaction,
  destructive scope outside the request, or a genuine blocker.
- Continue approved work through implementation, proportionate verification,
  scoped commit, and the repository's guarded integration lifecycle.
- Do not widen scope, mutate external systems, or delegate merely to avoid doing
  the work locally.
- A completion claim includes evidence. If verification is impossible, name the
  failed check, attempted alternatives, and remaining uncertainty.
