# TKA ↔ SpiroAnim bridge

What works today, what it would take to generalize, and why it is shaped this
way. Companion to `README.md` (the transcription) in this folder.

## What exists

Every one of the 72 Eight Step base-cell sequences in the library carries two
fields:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| `spiroanimCell` | `1-AA` |
| `spiroanimUrl` | `https://spiroanim.com/player?r=ww08Ee11Y&p0=…&m0=…&p1=…&c=_i_bhq&v=6` |

Opening that URL loads the exact pattern in SpiroAnim's player. Verified live
on spiroanim.com 2026-08-09.

## How the URLs were made

**With his encoder, not a reimplementation.** The links were generated by
running a spec inside a checkout of `rbgirard/spiroanim` that calls
`useSpiroAnimQS(VDEF, useBaseQS(VDEF, { charset: CHARSET }), 6).encodeQS()` on
each `createEightStepPreviewAnimation({ reference, shape: 'diamond',
reversePlane: false })`. Every link was then fed back through his `decodeQS`
and re-encoded; all 72 round-trip byte-identically.

`docs/QUERY_STRING_FORMAT.md` states plainly that field order, group order,
group length, bit width, and the alphabet are persisted-data contracts across
six versions. Hand-rolling a v6 bit-packer in TKA would fork that contract and
silently rot on his next version. Generating with his code is the only correct
way to produce these, and it is why the link table is a static artifact
(`eightstep-deep-links.json`) rather than a TKA runtime encoder.

To regenerate: clone spiroanim, `npm install`, add the spec, run it, replace
the JSON, re-point `spiroanimUrl` on the 72 docs.

## Route matters — use `/player`

His router (`src/router/index.ts`) serves one `/app` component under a set of
aliases: `/player`, `/timeline`, `/editor`, `/concepts`, `/vulkantechgospel`,
`/quarterspacing`, `/8-step`, plus every two-pane split (`/play-edit`,
`/8stp-play`, …) generated by `paneSplits` in `useMainRoute.ts`.

Two failures found by testing, both worth knowing before building anything else:

1. **Do not link to `/`.** The landing page gates on an ENTER button, and after
   entering, the app restores the visitor's last pane layout. If that layout
   includes a concept pane, the pane regenerates its own animation from its
   selected cell and **overwrites the animation in the URL**. Observed: a link
   with `p0=S1__f.mD…` landed on `/play-vtg` with `p0=S__.blEs8…`.
2. **`/play` is a 404.** The short keys (`play`, `vtg`, `8stp`) only exist in
   two-pane combinations. The single-pane alias is the long form, `/player`.

So the deep-link target is `https://spiroanim.com/player?<encoded>`. A
single-pane player has no concept pane to clobber the payload.

## What a general bridge needs

Today only these 72 curated patterns can be linked, because their URLs were
generated ahead of time. Linking an arbitrary TKA sequence to SpiroAnim needs
one of:

- **A published encoder.** His codec extracted to a package TKA can depend on.
  Then any TKA sequence with 90° hand arcs and integer turns becomes a link.
- **An interchange format.** A small readable JSON contract (per-frame hand
  point, prop point, turns, arc) that both apps read and write. His
  `AnimReadableFunc.encodeReadable`/`decodeReadable` is already almost exactly
  this shape internally, and it is the layer his own concept builders emit
  before compilation.

The second is the smaller ask and the more durable one. It is also what he
said he is open to: a communication protocol, as opposed to changes to his
layout. Nothing in either option requires him to accept UI changes.

## Direction: SpiroAnim → TKA

The reverse already works offline and needs nothing from him. His compiled
frames resolve to TKA letters through `rootCompile()` + `closestPoint()` and
the pictograph dataframes — the method in `README.md`, proven at 100% over
8,640 steps. A live version would need his codec in TKA (same dependency as
above) to decode a pasted spiroanim URL into a TKA sequence.

The v9 editor artifact in `editor-v9-quarter-turn-club-loop.json` proves this
path is not limited to the integer-turn concept builders. The editor's
45-degree frame arcs pair cleanly into Level 4 quarter-turn motions. TKA's
continuous animator already accepts the resulting 0.25-turn values; the bridge
record keeps explicit interradial endpoints so persistence and rendering do not
lose that geometry.

## Cell-identity bridge route

Shipped: `/from/spiroanim/<cellKey>`. SpiroAnim links out with a cell identity
rather than an animation payload, and the Composer resolves that identity
against the vendored transcription. No codec crosses the boundary, so neither
app can rot the other's format.

### The cellKey grammar

```
<concept>.<reference>.<ratio>.<shape>.<variant>[.o<degrees>]
```

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| `concept` | `vtg` \| `qtr` \| `8stp` |
| `reference` | vtg/qtr `^[1-6]-[1-6]$`; 8stp `^[1-8]-(aa\|ae\|ai\|ea\|ee\|ei\|ia\|ie\|ii)$` |
| `ratio` | `1x1` \| `1x2` \| `1x3` \| `1x4` \| `1x5` \| `2x3` \| `2x5` (`x` replaces `:`, which a path segment cannot carry cleanly) |
| `shape` | `diamond` \| `box` |
| `variant` | `base` \| `anti` (`anti` ⇔ `isAnti: true` in the transcription) |
| `o<degrees>` | optional pattern orientation: `o-90` \| `o-45` \| `o0` \| `o45` \| `o90` \| `o180`. vtg/qtr only. |

Example: `vtg.1-1.1x1.diamond.base`, or with an orientation
`vtg.3-4.1x3.diamond.base.o45`.

The key is all-lowercase. His data spells the 8-Step rows uppercase (`1-AA`),
so the boundary lowercases them and one spelling crosses the wire. 8-Step has
no speed-ratio axis: its key is always `1x1`, and a non-`1x1` 8-Step key is a
wrong key rather than a key for some other cell.

Five fields address the canonical reading of a cell. The transcription carries
two further axes the key does not (`quarters` on qtr, `reversePlane` on 8stp);
his own link builder emits `quarters: 1` / `reversePlane: false`, which is also
what `cell-catalogue.json` records, so the bridge addresses that reading. The
3,312 transcription entries collapse to 2,160 addressable keys.

### Speed ratios: seven bridged, one excluded

SpiroAnim's ratio picker offers 1:1, 2:1, 1:2, 1:3, 2:3, 1:4, 1:5 and 2:5.
The original transcription (2026-08-09) captured only 1:1, 1:3 and 1:5. The
other four TKA-readable ratios were derived on 2026-09-01 by compiling every
VTG cell at every picker ratio through his own compiler
(`createDefaultVtgAnimation` → `rootCompile` → `closestPoint`) and reading
three facts off the output:

1. **Hand paths are identical within a ratio family.** 1:2 lands on the same
   grid points as 1:1 in every interval; 1:4 and 2:3 match 1:3; 2:5 matches
   1:5 (72/72 cells each). The ratio only changes how far the prop turns.
2. **A two-cycle ratio (2:3, 2:5) repeats its 8-interval hand path exactly.**
   His compiler emits 17 frames for it, and intervals 9–16 reproduce 1–8, so
   the TKA word is the one-cycle word written twice (8 steps).
3. **Prop rotation per 45° interval is ±45 · den/num.** In TKA terms, turns
   per step = (den/num − 1)/2 for both hands: 1:2 → 0.5, 1:4 → 1.5, 2:3 →
   0.25, 2:5 → 0.75. The 1:1/1:3/1:5 entries already carried 0/1/2 by the
   same formula, so the derivation is turn substitution on the family entry,
   not a second transcription.

| Ratio | Family | Turns / step | Steps | Default view |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1:1 | — | 0 | 4 | 0 |
| 1:2 | 1:1 | 0.5 | 4 | -90 |
| 1:3 | — | 1 | 4 | 0 |
| 1:4 | 1:3 | 1.5 | 4 | -90 |
| 1:5 | — | 2 | 4 | 0 |
| 2:3 | 1:3 | 0.25 | 8 | -90 |
| 2:5 | 1:5 | 0.75 | 8 | -90 |

**2:1 is excluded.** Its prop turns 22.5° per interval, i.e. 45° per 90° hand
arc — less rotation than a 0-turn pro shift, and not a float either (a float
is a separate binary state in which the prop holds its absolute angle, not a
negative turn count). TKA has no reading for it, so the resolver rejects a
`2x1` key and SpiroAnim's chip reports no cell for it.

TKA's quarter-turn support is real, not an approximation: the resolver writes
the fractional turns straight into each motion, and the end orientation comes
from the canonical radial fractional-turn cycle (`orientation.ts`).

**Forward compatibility:** unrecognised dot-separated fields beyond the fifth
are IGNORED, not rejected. A future SpiroAnim may append an axis without
breaking every Composer build already deployed. A RECOGNISED trailing field is
parsed strictly: an `o` token with a value outside the set above, or a second
`o` token, is a wrong coordinate and fails the parse. Anything else malformed
resolves to `null`. Never a guess.

### Orientation translation

SpiroAnim's "Pattern rotation" control turns the whole flower; TKA has no such
global knob, so the bridge translates it into the positions themselves. Three
facts, all verified by compiling cells inside his own checkout on 2026-08-30:

1. The transcription corpus was captured at pattern orientation **-90**, the
   universal default at capture time (2026-08-09).
2. His current default is per-ratio (`getDefaultVtgPatternOrientation`): **0**
   for the odd-denominator one-cycle ratios (1:1, 1:3, 1:5) and **-90** for
   the even-denominator and two-cycle ratios (1:2, 1:4, 2:3, 2:5), vtg and
   qtr alike. So a key without an `o` token means "what a SpiroAnim user sees
   by default" for that ratio: 90° clockwise of the raw transcription for
   1:3, the raw transcription itself for 1:4. The resolver mirrors that rule
   in `defaultSpiroAnimOrientation()`.
3. Each +45° of orientation moves every hand exactly one compass step
   **clockwise** (n → ne → e → …). Both apps label the compass from the same
   screen frame — the transcribed grid points came from his `closestPoint`
   mapping — so the convention carries over verbatim.

The resolver (`orientation-rotation.ts`) rotates each transcribed step's
positions clockwise by `(requested − (−90)) / 45` compass steps before the
dataframe lookup. 45° views land on intercardinal points and resolve against
the Box dataframe; the resolver's merged Diamond+Box index already covers that
(proven by the o45 sweep over all 2,016 vtg/qtr cells). Turns are per-hand
scalars and survive rotation unchanged, so the derived word is
rotation-invariant. **8stp has no orientation axis:** it never rotates, and an
`o` token on an 8stp key is treated as a foreign field.

Return links follow the same rule: `vtg-qtr-deep-links.json` holds each cell
at its ratio's default orientation — 0 for 1:1/1:3/1:5 (byte-verified against
a live 0-orientation URL), -90 for the four ratios added on 2026-09-01 — which
is the default view the Composer renders for a plain key.

### Return links are vendored, not encoded

The trip back is two static maps, because his query codec is version-aware and
the two catalogues were exported at different versions:

| Artifact | Keyed by | Codec version |
|---|---|---|
| `vtg-qtr-deep-links.json` | cellKey | current (v11) |
| `eightstep-deep-links.json` | uppercase row label (`1-AA`) | legacy v6 |

Both were generated by running his encoder inside a spiroanim checkout and
round-tripping every URL through his own `decodeQS`, the same method as the
72 Eight Step links above. His decoder still reads v6, so the legacy map stays
as it is rather than being re-encoded; TKA holds no copy of the codec, and
guessing one produces links that look right and open the wrong animation.

Both catalogues are diamond-only and the 8-Step export has no anti variant. A
cell with no vendored link simply has no link and the route omits the button. A
vendored value that is not a `https://spiroanim.com/player?` URL is treated as
a missing entry. Coverage today: 1,080 of 1,080 addressable diamond cells
(the 576 links for the four ratios added on 2026-09-01 were generated with
`createVtgPreviewAnimation` / `createQtrPreviewAnimation` at orientation -90
and the generator was byte-checked against all 432 existing links first).

### Error behavior

The route never throws and never guesses. A malformed key, a well-formed key
with no transcription entry, or a step with no matching pictograph all resolve
to the same honest state: a card reading "No bridge entry for `<cellKey>`. This
link may come from a newer SpiroAnim version," with a way back to SpiroAnim and
a way into the Composer. No redirect, no substitute sequence.

The page is `noindex` and client-rendered: resolution reads the pictograph
dataframes from `/data/pictographs/*.csv`, which has no server-side path.

## Constraints to respect

He is explicit that any UI must resize to small resolutions and work at large
ones, adapt portrait/landscape, and work within his existing pane layout, which
he is not changing. Any contribution assumes his layout as fixed and fits
inside a pane.
