# Three Descriptive Words

Biomechanics. Notation. Composition.

## Rationale

- **Biomechanics** — the body angle. Rare in art+tech submissions. Signals the research involves physical reality, not abstract theory.
- **Notation** — the concrete tool. Signals formal system, not loose documentation.
- **Composition** — the collaborative use. Same word music uses for the same activity. Captures performers building acts together with shared scaffolding, without resorting to the generic "collaboration."

Three nouns, all specific, all rare in LACMA submissions. Covers body (research) → tool (notation) → use (composition) in order.
