# What are the main influences upon your work as an artist? How does your past work inform your current project?

The Museum of Jurassic Technology in Los Angeles presents real and fabricated exhibits with zero distinction between them. Its founder David Wilson received a MacArthur fellowship for an institution most visitors cannot fully verify. That commitment to ambiguity is the model for The Kinetic Archive's tone.

The Stanley Parable demonstrated that a walking sim can be funny and structurally inventive without combat or puzzles. Andy Kaufman proved that the audience's inability to locate the boundary between performance and reality is itself the art form. Both inform how the museum presents its history.

My past work is the foundation. I created the Kinetic Alphabet in 2022 because my circus troupe needed a way to notate choreography for synchronized performances. That system grew into published books, a card game, and a web application. The notation system runs inside the game. My past work is the engine for this project.
