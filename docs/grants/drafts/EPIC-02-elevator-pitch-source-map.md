**Portal question:** Project elevator pitch

**Purpose:** State what the player experiences and the idea that makes this
specific game memorable. Do not spend the limited space on the funding request,
equipment, production method, or general praise.

## Verified story facts

The current story bible establishes that:

- the player walks through an abandoned government archive;
- the institution presents the Kinetic Alphabet's fabricated history as fact;
- the history begins in prehistory and moves through later eras;
- the institution became the Bureau of Kinetic Containment and collapsed in
  2003;
- the player learns through exhibits, physical spaces, and optional details;
- the notation and Flow Arts practice underneath the fiction are real;
- the game is a first-person narrative experience, not a training simulator.

These are fact sources, not permission to copy the story bible's prose into the
application.

## Recommended 225-character angle

1. Lead with the player entering an abandoned bureaucratic museum or archive.
2. State that the institution treats the Kinetic Alphabet as something that has
   shaped human history since prehistory.
3. Make clear that the player learns the real notation system while uncovering
   the institution's collapse.

Save these details for later fields:

- friends and community members portraying historical practitioners;
- mocap and 3D locomotion production;
- the proof-of-concept argument for Flow Arts in video games;
- the full reality-bending science-fiction plot;
- the educational design and historical-era structure.

## Wording guardrails

- Use "walking sim" or "first-person narrative game," not "walkable
  simulation."
- Show that the player learns a real system without labeling the game an
  educational product.
- Do not imply that the fictional historical figures are real practitioners.
- Do not say the game is already built in Unreal Engine.
- Do not use generic claims about innovation, immersion, or changing games.
