# How is this a catalytic moment in your practice? How will your proposed project act as a catalyst for your artistic and professional growth?

I have been performing fire and teaching flow arts for nine years. In 2022 my circus troupe needed a way to notate choreography, so I made one. The Kinetic Alphabet turned into published books and a card game. I taught myself to code so I could build a web application for it. That app launches publicly this month.

Every piece I have built has pulled me further from performing and into building tools for a field that has almost no infrastructure. The game is the next piece. The story bible is written. The narrative design is resolved. I know what I want to build and I know how to learn what I need to learn. I do not know Unreal Engine 5 yet.

I have built everything so far by myself. This project needs more than just me.
