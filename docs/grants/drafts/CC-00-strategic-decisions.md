# Creative Capital: Strategic Decisions for Austen

## Discipline Selection

**Recommendation: Visual Arts > Technology**

The project is a video game. Your reviewer should be someone who understands technology-driven art, not a dance curator wondering why a flow artist is making a game. Creative Capital explicitly welcomes technology as a sub-discipline within Visual Arts and showed examples of tech-driven awardees (Rachel Rossin, Brian House).

Your performing arts background is part of the story (Q2: Influences, Q4: Catalytic Moment), but the PROPOSED WORK is a technology art project. Match the discipline to the project, not the resume.

**Alternative:** If you feel strongly that your resume reads more as performing arts and your work sample is a performance video rather than a screen recording, Performing Arts > Dance could work. But you'd get a dance reviewer evaluating a video game proposal. Risky.

## Work Sample

**Recommendation: Screen recording of TKA Scribe**

Record a 3-5 minute continuous screen capture of you building a sequence in TKA Scribe. Show the full flow: pick a starting position, add beats, watch the animation play. No cuts. No trailer. Drop the reviewer into the action (their exact advice from the info session).

This is your strongest option because:
1. It directly demonstrates the notation system the game is built around
2. It shows a completed, functioning application (proves you can execute)
3. It connects your past work to the proposed project (through line)
4. A technology reviewer will understand and appreciate what they're seeing

**Alternative:** A fire performance video. This shows Austen-as-performer but doesn't connect as directly to the proposed project. Use this only if Visual Arts > Technology isn't the selected discipline.

**Important:** The work sample must be a COMPLETED PAST WORK. TKA Scribe qualifies. The museum game does not (it's the proposed project). Do not submit concept art or story bible excerpts as the work sample.

## What NOT to Do

1. Don't apply in Performing Arts with a screen recording work sample. Mismatch.
2. Don't mention "tutorial videos" or "educational content." CC explicitly does not fund projects whose main purpose is educational.
3. Don't describe TKA Scribe as the proposed project. The app exists already. The proposed project is the GAME.
4. Don't overexplain flow arts. The reviewer doesn't need a primer. They need to be excited about your project.
5. Don't mention the DCASE or FTFA grants. Not relevant here.

## Timeline Alignment

- CC projects cannot premiere before July 1, 2027. The museum game is years from completion. Perfect fit.
- CC prefers early-stage projects. The game has a story bible but no 3D assets. Perfect fit.
- CC anticipates completion within 5 years. Ambitious but achievable for a multi-year passion project.
