# Dialogue with Technology / Culture (100-word max)

## Exact Prompt
"In what ways does your project inspire dialogue about the relationship between technology and culture or other relevant issues?"

## Draft

The Kinetic Alphabet is a finite, computational notation that encompasses a significant part of the flow arts landscape. Every position, transition, and variation in that scope is enumerated. The question it invites is what it means to complete the codification of a physical practice. Sequences can now be generated, stored, printed, and shared, instead of living only as videos on social feeds. Composition becomes possible between performers who never meet. Teaching and learning become more efficient, whether instructor-led or self-directed. Because the notation is computable, it's where 3D avatars, the museum, and the rest of the art/technology fusion start.
