# Public Engagement Plan (100-word max)

## Exact Prompt
"Describe your proposed plan for public engagement. What opportunities do you foresee to share prototypes, demonstrations, and processes with the public?"

## Draft

The notation app is online, with a small early user base. The choreography cards in development are the physical entry point: a way to handle the notation before opening the software. I teach and perform at flow arts festivals at least three times a year, and will demonstrate the upgraded avatars and cards in those contexts during the grant. The routing dataset, vocabulary, and codebase will release openly for researchers and technologists. LACMA's 2027 Symposium and 2028 Demo Day offer major opportunities to present the work publicly.
