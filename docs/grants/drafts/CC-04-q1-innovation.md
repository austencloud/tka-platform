# How does your project take an original and imaginative approach to content and form?

No video game has taken flow arts as its subject. The Kinetic Archive attempts something specific: present a fabricated history so sincerely that the player cannot locate where fiction ends and reality begins.

The museum's audio narrator and its handwritten annotator are the same person at different stages of life. The player experiences both voices for the entire game without knowing this. On a second playthrough, every line is recontextualized. 
The game also bridges fiction and function. The notation system displayed in the museum is a working tool that exists outside the game. Players who finish The Kinetic Archive can open the real web app and use the same system the museum claims is ancient. The fiction does not end. It becomes optional.
