# Artistic/Creative Merit (100-word max)

I created the Kinetic Alphabet, the most comprehensive notation system flow arts has ever had. The form existed for decades without it. The artistic merit lies in giving an uncodified practice the same kind of foundation music and dance have long had, then extending it with tools that make the form teachable, composable, and transmissible: 3D avatars performers can watch, printed choreography cards they can arrange, and notated files they can share. Flow artists can write acts they could not have written before, and see them performed before the first rehearsal.
