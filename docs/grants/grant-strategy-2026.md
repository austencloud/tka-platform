## Framing (adapt emphasis per funder)

"I built the first notation system for movement arts. It works. People use it. Now I'm building the cultural context around it -- a narrative game that treats flow arts with the institutional seriousness they've never received."

- For arts funders: notation system, cultural preservation, movement literacy
- For tech funders: working software, novel application, Unreal Engine 5
- For game funders: narrative walking sim, UE5, Museum of Jurassic Technology meets Stanley Parable

## Active Deadlines

### Creative Capital Award -- up to $50,000
- Portal open NOW (March 2, 2026)
- DEADLINE: April 2, 2026
- Unrestricted project grant + professional development
- Explicitly funds "technology" as a discipline
- Individual artists, all 50 states
- https://creative-capital.org/creative-capital-award/award-application/

### LACMA Art + Technology Lab -- up to $50,000
- DEADLINE: April 22, 2026
- 3-5 projects selected, two-year duration
- Partners include Anthropic, Snap, Hyundai
- Open worldwide to individuals and collectives
- https://www.lacma.org/art/lab/grants

### Epic MegaGrants -- $5,000 to $150,000
- Window: June 29 - September 4, 2026
- Specifically for UE5 projects, solo devs welcome
- No IP transfer, no forced milestones, no policed spending
- You keep everything
