## Build Order

1. Stavvy (starter — establishes creature behavior pattern)
2. Orbelle (different movement family — validates system works for non-staff props)
3. One Tier 2, one Tier 3 (prove rarity/difficulty scaling)
4. Expand from there

## Design Rules

- Every creature's behavior comes from real prop physics
- No invented animal hybrids — props ARE the creatures
- Personality is movement, not backstory
- Googly eyes optional per art direction decision
