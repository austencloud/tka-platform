# Flow Arts Software off-page action kit

Updated 2026-07-20. This kit turns independent reputation into scheduled work.
It does not assume that on-page or technical SEO is finished. Indexing, product
quality, published evidence, and earned references develop together.

The canonical commercial target is:

`https://tkaflowarts.com/composer`

The informational target is:

`https://tkaflowarts.com/roots/software`

Send product-intent references to `/composer`. Send history, taxonomy, and
research references to `/roots/software`. Do not create or promote a second
commercial landing page for the same phrase.

## Campaign rules

- Earn coverage by giving the publisher something useful to verify.
- Disclose the relationship to TKA in community posts and review outreach.
- Ask for honest experience. Never require positive sentiment.
- Let editors choose their wording and anchor text.
- Record declines and stop after one reasonable follow-up.
- Correct factual errors without turning every correction into a link demand.
- Track what referred visitors do inside the product.

Link attributes are not a scorecard. A relevant community reference may drive
qualified readers even when it carries `nofollow` or `ugc`. A followed link on a
random directory may do nothing useful. Evaluate the page, audience, referral
traffic, and product activation.

## Execution order

### 1. Prepare the source pack

Outreach starts only when the receiving editor can verify the pitch. Keep one
folder or public page with:

- The canonical category statement and product URL.
- Current screenshots and a short demo video.
- A factual creator bio with approved headshot and contact details.
- Pricing, platform support, release notes, privacy information, and known
  limitations.
- A short description for product references and a longer description for
  articles.
- Permission notes for every quote, image, and case study.

Review this pack before each campaign. Old screenshots and inflated claims make
earned coverage harder.

### 2. Reclaim existing references

Start with pages that already identify Austen or The Kinetic Alphabet. A direct
source link is a reasonable editorial correction when it helps the reader verify
the reference.

Current research seeds include:

- [Fund the Flow Arts grant-recipient announcement](https://fundtheflowarts.org/announcing-2024-25-seed-fund-grant-recipients/)
- [Flow Fest Chicago workshop and instructor bios](https://flowfests.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Flow-Fest-Chicago-Workshops-Bios.pdf)

Before contacting anyone, confirm that the live page still contains the mention
and identify the most useful target. Use `/composer` for the software. Use the
homepage or a sourced background page when the mention is about the broader
project.

Signed outreach template:

> Subject: Source link for the TKA reference on [page]
>
> Hi [name],
>
> The [article/program page] mentions The Kinetic Alphabet. Its current source
> page is [canonical URL]. If a direct source would help readers verify the
> reference, that is the URL to use.
>
> The existing wording does not need to change. Thanks for including the work.
>
> Austen Cloud

This is a source request, not a demand for a keyword-rich link.

### 3. Publish practitioner case studies

Recruit people who have used Composer in a real teaching, rehearsal, or creation
setting. Capture evidence before drafting the page:

- The problem they were trying to solve.
- How the product fit into the work.
- An observable result or artifact.
- Friction, limitations, and changes they would request.
- Permission for names, quotes, images, and links.

Publish each case study on a stable URL. Send the finished page to the participant
for factual approval. Sharing remains optional, and access or compensation must
never depend on a positive review.

Signed invitation template:

> Subject: Documenting how you used Flow Arts Composer
>
> Hi [name],
>
> Your use of Composer for [specific activity] could help other practitioners
> understand where the tool fits. The case study would cover the process, the
> result, and any parts that got in the way. Nothing needs to be flattering.
>
> Participation includes factual approval before publication. Would a 25-minute
> recorded conversation work during [date range]?
>
> Austen Cloud

### 4. Publish original research

The recurring anchor asset is a **State of Flow Arts Software** report. Build it
as research rather than a promotional survey.

Required publication material:

1. Research question and survey instrument.
2. Recruitment channels and collection dates.
3. Response count, exclusions, and analysis method.
4. Findings with charts that can be cited independently.
5. Limitations, funding or conflicts, and a corrections path.
6. An anonymized dataset when consent and privacy rules allow it.

The report should include competing tools fairly. Composer can appear in the
findings without being declared the winner. Send the report to instructors,
festivals, flow organizations, prop businesses, and movement-notation
researchers whose readers can use the data.

Signed research pitch:

> Subject: New data on how flow artists use software
>
> Hi [name],
>
> The State of Flow Arts Software report surveyed [verified count] people about
> [research question]. The full instrument, method, limitations, and charts are
> published at [report URL].
>
> [One finding stated with its denominator] may be relevant to your coverage of
> [specific topic]. The chart and source link can be used with attribution.
>
> Austen Cloud

Never insert a number until the published report supports it.

### 5. Build the video source trail

Record videos around complete product workflows and questions people already
ask. Each important video needs a page on tkaflowarts.com with:

- A stable title and thumbnail.
- A transcript edited for accuracy.
- Links to the product surface and related documentation.
- Publication and update dates that match the media.
- `VideoObject` data only when every marked-up field is visible and correct.

Publish through the TKA channel, then offer the specific video to instructors or
event organizers when it supports their existing material. Avoid generic embed
requests.

### 6. Run targeted editorial outreach

Build a small list by reader fit. Research every target page before sending a
message.

- **Flow organizations and festivals:** Offer research, a workshop source, or a
  practitioner case study for a resource page or editorial article.
- **Instructors and educators:** Offer a demonstrated teaching workflow or an
  accurate tutorial for lesson resources and course notes.
- **Prop makers and retailers:** Offer evidence about planning or documenting
  practice for buyer education or a learning-resource page.
- **Juggling and movement-notation communities:** Offer a sourced comparison,
  interactive example, or research data for a technical article or resource
  collection.
- **Creative-tool publications:** Offer a product story with a working demo and
  documented release for a review, interview, or tool roundup.

Each pitch must name the exact page or audience fit. A bulk template with the
recipient name swapped in does not meet this standard.

### 7. Participate in communities without staging consensus

Reddit, forums, Discord servers, and social groups can introduce the work to
people who care about it. Participate under a real identity and disclose the TKA
connection.

Good participation answers the question in the post, shows the relevant artifact,
and links only when the source adds material the answer cannot fit. Bad
participation drops the product URL, coordinates votes, hides the relationship,
or creates several accounts that appear independent.

Community mentions are discovery and feedback. They are not a substitute for
independent editorial coverage.

### 8. Use directories and entity databases selectively

Submit Composer only to maintained directories with accurate categories and
real users. Keep the description factual and point to `/composer`. Skip bulk
submission services.

Use `sameAs` only for official profiles that exist and are maintained. Wikidata
comes after enough independent reliable sources exist to support an item under
its own policies. Verify every proposed statement before submission. Wikidata
does not promise a Google result or AI citation.

### 9. Use launch moments when there is news

Product Hunt, Show HN, newsletters, podcasts, and trade coverage work best when
there is a release, research result, public dataset, or striking demonstration.
Schedule a launch around that evidence. A listing by itself is not the campaign.

## Measurement setup

Create the baseline before the first outreach batch.

### Search visibility

In Search Console, track `/composer`, `/roots/software`, branded queries, and the
defined Flow Arts Software query group. Confirm the generative AI inclusion
setting when it reaches the property. Review the Generative AI performance report
when available.

### Outreach ledger

Record:

- Publisher and exact source page.
- Contact name and relationship history.
- Proposed reader value.
- Target URL.
- First contact, one follow-up, response, and publication date.
- Link or mention context and any declared sponsorship.
- Referral sessions and product activation.

### Product outcome

The off-page campaign earns its keep when referred and organic visitors open
Composer and use it. Track the agreed activation, save/share, and return-use
events. Do not report outreach volume or raw link count as the main result.

Review the ledger monthly. Expand the sources that send engaged users or credible
citations. Stop tactics that produce only listings, spam complaints, or empty
traffic.

## Prohibited tactics

- Purchased links without the required sponsorship disclosure and attributes.
- Private blog networks or expired-domain link schemes.
- Fake reviews, review gating, or rewards tied to positive sentiment.
- Invented third-party quotes, seeded consensus, or undisclosed sockpuppet posts.
- Exact-match anchor demands and reciprocal-link quotas.
- Bulk guest posts or directory submissions made only for links.
- Doorway pages, query-variant page factories, or AI-written filler.
- `llms.txt` presented as a Google AI-ranking requirement.
- Wikidata statements without reliable sources.

Google's current position is that ordinary Search fundamentals govern its AI
features. The campaign therefore produces material that readers, editors, and
practitioners choose to reference. See [Google's generative-search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)
and [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
