# Flow Fest Sim Gate 5 integration contract

Gate 5 integrates the complete Thursday journey without changing the approved measured terrain, registered routes, traced connectors, camera controller, Rapier player, or EUC movement owner.

## Runtime route

`/test/flow-fest-sim` is the player route. `/test/flow-fest-sim?gate5=1&capture=1` adds three clearly named review-staging controls for exact registered landmarks. Those controls are evidence tooling. They do not replace locomotion or claim timed vehicle travel.

## Integrated state

The source-locked journey journal observes the authoritative mobility pose and records settled landmark entries only. It recognizes the lower gate, selected branch camp, west upper parking, and festival. Transit space does not create synthetic edges.

Acceptance requires:

- selected camp branch survives reload;
- no staging or hydration frame creates a phantom crossing;
- camp-to-festival and festival-to-camp establish a reverse traversal;
- leaving and re-entering the festival increments the re-entry proof;
- the final return reaches the selected camp with the completed fire jam intact.

## Sound boundaries

One long-lived procedural graph crossfades arrival, woodland, camp, fire, LED, and crowd layers. It does not restart sources at zone boundaries. Browser autoplay policy remains intact: loading or reloading never starts audio without a gesture.

## Evidence interpretation

Gate 5 combines three kinds of proof:

1. Gate 2 continuous route and dynamic-collider replays prove the walkable terrain between registered landmarks.
2. Gate 4 interaction footage proves the wheel-to-foot and fire-jam slice.
3. Gate 5 browser captures prove the integrated state, audio boundaries, backtracking, re-entry, and reload behavior.

The composite walk video identifies those sources. It is not presented as one uninterrupted real-time trip or as timed vehicle simulation.
