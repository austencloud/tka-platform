Water: the drowned gallery and the grotto

Every number on this page was read out of the room's own geometry, not drawn by eye. Second version: the viewing deck is gone, the last look now happens in the doorway you leave through, and the water in front of each case behaves differently so the three of them stop reading as one thing repeated.

The wing is one walk with no branches. You wade in, you go under, you come up, and then you meet three performers across still water you cannot cross. The walk is 124.6 m long; 28.4 m of it is spent with your head under water. The exhibit itself is the last 59.3 m.

S-01 · What it does vertically

This is the same walk seen from the side, flattened out straight. The black line is the floor under your feet; the orange dashed line is your eye. Where the orange line drops below the blue water surface, you are under. The shaded band marks that stretch.

The moment worth protecting is the landing at −3.1 m. It is not an authored number: it is the floor height that puts your eye exactly on the water surface, so the surface break happens standing still on a flat step rather than somewhere on a ramp.

The barrier is the water

The channel in front of the performers is 4.0 m wide and 2.25 m deep. It is on the blocked list, so the game will not let you walk into it, and the shelf the performers stand on is blocked too. There is a rail along the near edge as well. You cannot get to them by accident or on purpose.

A case is three things and one clock

The performer stands on the shelf at z 5.0. Their screen hangs on the wall 2.6 m to their left so it never hides them. Their card sits on the rail in front of you at 0.7 m high, low enough that it blocks nothing. One clock drives the performer and the screen together, so the pictograph on the wall is always the step the body is on.

Nothing doubles back

You come in at the south, go west, north, east along the cases, and leave through the east wall. Every metre of the route is walked once. The apron is wide enough (4.5 m) that the opener never stands in the way of the arrival view.

The last look is taken from the way out

An earlier version of this board put a viewing deck out over the pool with a "stand here" marked on it. That was wrong and it is gone. Nobody walks to a spot in a corner just to stand in it, and the three-across view was already available from the walk along the water — so the deck was asking for a second look at something you had just had.

The room already has one place every single visitor stops at, facing the right way: the carved threshold in the east wall. It is the only way out. It is 2.5 m wide, and standing in it the three cases sit behind your left shoulder inside a 39.1° view, receding: C is 12.7 m away and nearest, A is 21.4 m away and smallest. The ending is the leaving. Nothing has to be built for it.

Each one is still doubled from there. With the eye at 1.30 m, the line to a figure's reflected head crosses the surface at z 10.1 m for all three, inside the channel's 6.3–10.3 m span. C's bounce lands at x 22.5, only 0.3 m from the channel's east edge — it works, but it is the tight one, and it is the first thing to check in the graybox.

Three cases, three surfaces

The water in front of each case is the same body of water, treated three ways. It is decoration and nothing more — it is not a code, and no elemental word is spoken anywhere in this wing. What it buys is that the three cases stop being one box drawn three times, and that the room warms up as you walk toward the door you leave by.

Four calls I need from you

W-1

The performer shelf sits under the water line

The shelf A, B and C stand on is at -1 m. The grotto's water surface is at -0.45 m — 0.55 m higher. Right now no water is drawn over the shelf, so it reads as a dry ledge lower than the water beside it, which cannot happen.

Recommend

Flood it. Draw the water across the shelf too, so the three of them stand shin-deep in still water and the reflection starts at their ankles. It is the Water wing; performers standing in it is better than a dry ledge. Or raise the shelf to −0.35 m and keep them dry.

W-2

The water in front of each case behaves differently

As drawn before, the three cases were three identical boxes 7 m apart, and nothing but the figure inside told them apart. This gives each one its own surface: glass at A, a slow ripple at B, mist at C. It is decoration — it does not encode anything about the three sequences, and nothing is named out loud.

Recommend

Take it. It costs one shader parameter per case, it makes the three read as a progression rather than a repeat, and the steam at C sits 3 m from the Fire door, so the room hands you to the next wing instead of just ending. If it plays as a gimmick in the graybox, drop it and the room still works — nothing else depends on it.

W-3

The screens and cards are new objects

Three wall screens, three rail cards and the opener plinth do not exist in the geometry yet. Nothing else is being added — the parapet deck that was on the last version of this board is withdrawn. Their positions are fixed by this board; the objects get built with the shared graybox harness.

Recommend

Build them in the terrain module so the walkable geometry and the drawing stay one source, the way every other rect in this room already works.

W-4

The walk is long before anything happens

65.3 m of the 124.6 m walk is approach, drowning and surfacing, with one lit bloom in the middle. The exhibit itself is the last 59.3 m.

Recommend

Keep it. The submerged leg is what makes the grotto land, and it is already built. But if it plays long in the graybox, the west run is the leg to shorten.
