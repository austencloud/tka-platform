# Ember Gate 4 Meshy Geology Pass

Status: implemented and runtime verified

Authority: museum tracker `ZSnkB98pb0wz6PO17XKp`

Budget: 240 Meshy credits maximum

## Outcome

Replace Ember R6's primitive geological surface language without discarding its
continuous volcanic-world layout, responsive stage, performer relationship,
seventeen-point lava route, or complete orbit. Meshy supplies replaceable
geological modules. Blender remains the composition and material-integration
owner, and the runtime lava system remains the only motion owner for molten
flow.

## Registered target synthesis

The three studies under `evidence/gate-4-meshy-r1/targets/` are independent
reference axes rather than one painting to reproduce:

- `target-b-fractured-columnar-chasm.png` owns the approved Columnar Furnace
  silhouette: two asymmetric geological shoulders, broken cooling joints, and
  an offset distant aperture.
- `target-a-blackglass-caldera.png` owns near-field material scale: buried mass,
  collapsed shelves, vesicular basalt, blackglass, and talus.
- `target-c-breached-lava-delta.png` owns environmental depth and the perception
  that the lava system continues far beyond the performance shelf.

The locked R6 hero camera, three-performer read, 4.5 m action radius, front-stage
facing, and surrounding terrain remain invariant. The generated images do not
own coordinates.

## Asset lineup

Four isolated multi-view reference families live under
`assets/meshy-refs/ember/`. The first three produced two geometry candidates:

1. `hero-columnar-escarpment`: an eighteen-metre fractured cooling-joint mass
   with buried entablature, snapped groups, talus, and undercut ledges.
2. `collapsed-lava-bank`: a low bank of ropey folds, broken crust plates,
   collapsed tube structure, and rubble toe for the lava-river margins.
3. `obsidian-fumarole-talus`: a connected glassy talus and three-vent formation
   for foreground and middle-distance accents.
4. `distant-breached-caldera`: a low, open-rimmed caldera commissioned after
   runtime review exposed the procedural vent as a pyramid-like backdrop.

The first generation wave is geometry-only Meshy 7 multi-image work: six calls
at twenty credits, capped at 120. Candidates are judged without texture by
silhouette, completeness, backside quality, terrain integration, and absence of
architectural or ocean-like forms. Only selected geometry may advance to a
ten-credit PBR texturing pass. The remaining 90 credits are a hard reserve for
replacement geometry, retexturing, or remesh work; no automatic retry may cross
the 240-credit total.

The selected B candidate in each original family received one ten-credit
retexture and one five-credit official Meshy remesh after local decimation
damaged the textured hero. The corrective caldera received the same complete
geometry, retexture, and remesh chain. Final spend is 200 of 240 credits,
leaving 40 credits unspent.

## Integration rules

- Bury or intersect every selected source with authored terrain; no asset may
  sit on the ground as a collectible or isolated backdrop.
- Keep selected assets outside the protected action radius and preserve the
  existing minimum structural-clearance computation.
- Do not repeat a high-detail source where the duplicate silhouette is visible
  in the same camera.
- Re-author material response toward one Ember palette. Meshy texture output is
  source material, not final lighting.
- Keep distant terrain procedural and spend detail where the orbit camera can
  resolve it.
- Preserve raw candidates, task IDs, source images, and rejection evidence so
  every paid call is auditable and reversible.

## Verification

The pass is reviewable only when it has:

- a candidate contact sheet and explicit winner/rejection record;
- a Blender hero, detail, plan, and eight-sector orbit board;
- a build report naming every imported source and its transformation;
- an optimized Meshopt/KTX2 runtime GLB with triangle, texture, and size report;
- decisive runtime captures with one and four performers, clean console output,
  and the required viewport sweep;
- focused tests for paid-request safety, asset provenance, clearance, shared
  lava-path parity, and the shipping artifact contract.

The art bar is not "more detailed than R6." The bar is that the hero view no
longer reads as extruded primitives, the river reads as molten rock instead of
line art, and every orbit sector belongs to one geological world.
