# Ember Geological World Gate 3 Decision

Authority: museum tracker `s3cxnp6hOLBVQR5dDF42`

The three directions use the same R7 Meshy landmarks, performer shelf, lava
route, material families, registered cameras, lighting, and 4.5 m protected
action radius. Only the surrounding terrain topology changes.

| Direction | Score | Decision | Adversarial read |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Breached Caldera Terraces | 8.7 | Selected | The offset caldera, collapsed benches, and open breach give the stage a geological cause, establish distinct near/middle/far silhouettes, and preserve multiple routes out of frame. |
| Basalt Badlands | 7.8 | Rejected | The aggressive ribs produce strong isolated silhouettes, but they begin to read as another ring of placed props and do not explain the river as convincingly. |
| Collapsed Lava Delta | 7.2 | Rejected | The layered flow lobes support the river, but the terrain stays too low and smooth to erase the remaining graybox read at the decisive camera. |

The selected direction received one production correction after the first
registered render. The former 23 m flat moat was reduced to a 10.8 m action
floor, eight fractured crust plates now weld the responsive shelf into the
country, and local erosion bowls prevent the retained Meshy landmarks from
being clipped by the higher terraces. The rear-left proof camera was lifted
above the west bench after the first orbit board exposed a terrain intersection.

This selection advances the terrain topology, not one immutable image. The
Blackglass Inferno atmosphere, R7 geological landmarks, and shared runtime lava
remain independent owners that can continue to evolve without discarding the
spatial result.
