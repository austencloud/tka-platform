# Ember R9 surface ecology decision ledger

## Fixed comparison contract

All targets use the accepted R8 Breached Caldera Terraces geometry, Blackglass
Inferno lighting, four R7 Meshy landmarks, seventeen-point lava route, 4.5 m
action clearance, performer placement, and the same hero, front-right, left,
and rear cameras. Only the surface families and their spatial masks change.

The basin uses a smooth per-vertex four-family mask. It does not swap whole
terrain faces between unrelated materials. The performance shelf remains a
continuous geological surface, and cooled fissures receive textured crust
instead of an unlit black material.

## Recommendation

Advance **Fresh Rift synthesis**. It keeps Fresh Flow Field's young, coherent
lava crust as the world-wide base, uses fractured basalt on exposed scarps and
Meshy landmarks, retains windborne ash only in sheltered pockets, and borrows
Hydrothermal Rift's iron-rich response only along the lava river, banks, and
contact zones. Broad sulfur fields are deliberately rejected.

This is a registered visual target, not a claim that Ember is already AAA.
Production still has to prove smooth world-space masking in Three.js, KTX2
delivery, lava-distance contact light, full-orbit behavior, performer-count
stage growth, seven viewports, console health, and frame pacing. The QA river's
flat emissive surface and the R8 stage-to-terrain seam remain production issues;
they are not hidden by the material decision.
