## Composition

- The original close river remains dominant. Its native geometry, bank mask and 240 raft trajectories are preserved.
- A roughly 307 m eastern distributary leaves the upper river and descends 113 m to the south boundary.
- A thinner roughly 196 m upper branch leaves the existing source channel, traverses the upper flank and rejoins the eastern route after about 78 m of descent.
- A non-emissive western overflow uses the existing clinker surface treatment, suggesting an older channel without adding another bright stripe.
- Cooled, irregular shoulders anchor the active branches. Their width and thermal intensity vary along the route; broad dark terrain remains between them.

These branches are authored interpretations constrained to the unchanged DEM, not new Flowy simulator results. Every half-metre centreline sample descends. Native raycasts confirm all branch roots overlap the existing river. Added active surfaces clear the 4.5 m action envelope by at least 27.57 m. The terrain, bench, original river, ridge masses, talus and hidden scientific reference retain their original vertex digests, listed in `build-report.json`.

## Owners and delivery

`scripts/build-ember-tributaries.py` extends the Blender asset; the existing optimizer gains `--tributaries`. `ember-midflank-finish.ts` extends the shared material/animation owner used by worker and legacy renderers. No new loader, render loop, lights, dependencies, remote assets or paid generation are introduced.

The new rivers share the existing shader, with exported green/red vertex-color ratio controlling their subordinate heat. Exported longitudinal UV increases downstream, retaining the R2 direction correction. Sixty-four smaller rafts join the existing 240 in the same instanced draw. Reduced motion freezes the shared clock.

Native source: `blender/ember-mountain-tributaries-r1.blend`. Versioned delivery: `static/models/ember/ember-mountain-tributaries-r1.glb`. Canonical GLB is identical: 2,994,452 bytes, about 246 KB more than R2. Thirteen mesh primitives; the isolated shared-world preview reports 16–18 draws depending on the view.
