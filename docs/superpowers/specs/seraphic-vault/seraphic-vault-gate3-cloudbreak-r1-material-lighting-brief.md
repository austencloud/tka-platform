# Olive Cloudbreak Gate 3 Material and Lighting Brief

## Target

A quiet limestone refuge above a deep cloud sea. It should feel warm, open, and restorative: a place made extraordinary by sunlight, altitude, water, and weather rather than by temples or religious ornament.

## Material family

- **Main landmass:** warm ivory limestone with pale honey undertones, mineral bands, wind-softened erosion, and a few restrained hairline cracks. The outer edge should feel like one continuous geological shelf, not a manufactured platform.
- **Performance terrace:** dry pale stone from the same formation, subtly smoother through natural wear. Keep the center visually quiet and free of an obvious stage ring, inlay, shrine, or landmark.
- **Lagoon:** clear turquoise water contained by irregular natural rock. It should read as a shallow reflecting lagoon, not a swimming pool. The far sun creates one soft white-gold reflection path across the water.
- **Olive trees:** twisted silver-brown trunks with sparse silvery-green leaves. Their asymmetrical, wind-shaped crowns frame the open center without making a garden.
- **Distant mesas:** the same limestone family with broader irregular bases, small eroded shelves, minimal vegetation, and progressively softer detail with distance.
- **Waterfalls:** thin white-blue threads that dissolve into mist before reaching the cloud ocean.
- **Clouds:** dense layered ivory forms with cool blue-gray undersides, warm rim light, and distinct near, middle, and far depth.
- **Sun:** one natural white-gold solar disk with a readable core and atmospheric bloom. No graphic rings, spokes, drawn halo, ornamental rays, or plastic sphere shading.

## Light hierarchy

The far sun is the only dominant source. It supplies warm side and back light, pale gold stone edges, the lagoon reflection, cloud rims, and restrained shafts through mist. Cool sky and cloud bounce keep the lower third readable for a performer, props, and motion trails.

Use fresh late-morning warmth rather than an orange sunset. Volumetric light should be visible but soft enough that the sun still feels distant. The sun must remain beyond and clear of every mesa silhouette.

## Atmosphere and motion intent

Clouds move almost imperceptibly at different depths. Olive leaves flutter in small irregular pulses. Waterfall mist drifts into the cloud layer. The lagoon surface carries slow fine ripples. Nothing competes with the performer.

## Protected composition

The approved Gate 2 cameras, horizon, landmass silhouette, dry central terrace, two olive-tree positions, right-edge lagoon, four mesa positions and elevations, and far-sun position are binding. The dark scale marker and yellow graybox guides are construction evidence and do not appear in the finished target.

## Rejection conditions

- Castle, temple, column, arch, ruin, statue, feather, wing, shrine, or religious symbol
- Extra lagoons, formal gardens, dense groves, settlements, or fantasy cities
- Independent display-platform geometry or landforms that no longer feel geologically related
- A drawn, ringed, spoked, blocked, oversized, or toy-like sun
- Empty blue sky without cloud enclosure and atmospheric depth
- A busy central terrace or foreground effects that weaken prop and trail contrast
- Text, logos, borders, labels, or watermarks inside the registered target
