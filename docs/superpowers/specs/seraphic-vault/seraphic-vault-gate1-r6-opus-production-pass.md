# Olive Cloudbreak Gate 1 revision 6

Date: 2026-08-10  
Status: Ready for Austen's visual-comprehension review  
Review route: `https://127.0.0.1:5176/test/celestial-asset-catalog`

## Review basis

A low-effort Claude Code review using Opus 5 rated revision 5 at 3.5/10 and
returned it for another visual pass. Its strongest criticism matched Austen's
notes: the lagoon still exposed a rectangular treatment, the shelf read as one
tan material, the sun and shadows lacked authority, the falls disappeared into
their mesas, and the hero camera devoted too much attention to unfinished
foreground. The review explicitly preserved the two olives, measured layout,
lagoon position, worn path, production clouds, and stage scale.

Tracker proposal `9hS7oQNdNlVwHLMUvB8R` records this production pass. It is
provenance, not an approval decision. Austen's "go for it" authorized the work;
the result remains unratified until the visual review.

## Revision 6 corrections

The lagoon reflector now computes shoreline distance from the authored
15-point outline. Foam, shallow-water tint, and the wet bank follow the same
irregular footprint, so the rectangle no longer reappears through the shader.
A continuous scene-specific bank and wet-stone band replace the copied ring of
small rocks. The two retained CC0 clusters stay sparse and keep their distinct
silhouettes.

Directional sunlight now casts readable contact shadows from both olive trees,
the terrace, and the distant geology. A restrained cool fill preserves trunk
detail. Shelf surface, strata, terrace, path, lagoon bank, and mesas use separate
limestone values instead of one tan treatment. Mesa colors lighten with distance
to support atmospheric depth.

The sun is a small distant sky element rather than a nearby glowing ball. The
registered Front camera keeps both olive silhouettes, the full dry terrace, the
right-edge lagoon, and the four-bank horizon. Animated falls were moved in front
of their rock faces so the water is visible instead of being removed by depth
testing. The lagoon crest remains registered at the outer waterline.

## Intentional graybox boundary

The rear sanctuary remains a measured massing proof. Revision 6 improves its
lighting and the path relationship but does not invent a production facade.
Final erosion, opening detail, and surface language belong to the later model
pass after this front-to-rear composition is approved.
