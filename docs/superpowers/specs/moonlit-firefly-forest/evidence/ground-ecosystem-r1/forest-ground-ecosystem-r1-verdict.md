# Forest Ground Ecosystem R1 verdict

**Verdict:** accepted for production visual review.

The Forest floor now uses one habitat-driven ecosystem owner rather than a
sprinkled grass effect. Fifteen authored habitats support 6,620 populations
across eight summer species. Fine turf is geometry, because whole-plant photo
cards became opaque brush stamps at meadow distance. Broadleaf groundcover,
ferns, nettles, periwinkle, moss, and dandelions keep their scanned Poly Haven
PBR source material and GPU instancing.

The final live review rejected two intermediate builds. The first packed 10 to
17 scanned grass clusters into each tuft, producing black hedge islands. The
second reduced those stamps but retained the wrong silhouette. The accepted
build uses 5,253 tapered blade clumps, 7 to 12 blades per clump, with rooted
wind weights and habitat-specific summer colour.

The worn trail, stage core, camp shelf, mushroom colonies, trees, rocks, and
performance area remain protected. The verifier measured a 1.764 m minimum
path shoulder, 11.492 m minimum campfire distance, and all 15 habitats populated.
The 14,679,272-byte near-frame GLB remains under its 18 MiB contract and keeps
Draco, WebP, and GPU instancing. Gate 12 Night lighting was not changed.

Sources are CC0 Poly Haven models recorded in
`scripts/forest-ground-ecosystem-assets.json`. The accepted pass spent no Meshy
credits because the available scans were a stronger fit for ecological ground
cover than another generated prop.
