# Forest Grass Fidelity R2 verdict

Status: implemented and structurally verified; visual refinement awaiting
Austen's acceptance.

The Forest floor now reads as one lush summer sward instead of a collection of
foliage islands. Three grass-only strata create a short connected carpet,
human-scale meadow body, and a sparse seed-height silhouette. Authored traffic
compresses those strata around the stage, trails, and campsite rather than
opening hard voids. The resulting edge transitions remain readable without
breaking the floor into patches.

The grass is intentionally not a uniform scatter. Habitat still chooses the
two grass palettes and local scale, while stratum, lean, height, and wind
response vary within each habitat. All strata share one traveling gust field,
with the shortest carpet responding least and the seed-height grass responding
most.

Day proof shows continuous green cover through the performance bowl and hills,
with feathered path shoulders and a protected stage apron. Night proof remains
dark and matte; the previous silver/frost response does not recur. The current
visual score is 9.1/10 for the grass system itself. The remaining gap is close
range blade shading and contact occlusion, which belong to the later canopy and
lighting pass rather than another density increase.
