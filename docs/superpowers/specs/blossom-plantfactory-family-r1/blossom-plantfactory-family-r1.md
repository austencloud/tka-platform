# Blossom PlantFactory Family R1

## Purpose

Produce a six-tree visual approval board before any new tree enters the Blossom
runtime or its floor plan. This proof replaces the rejected Meshy tree direction
with editable PlantFactory stock graphs authored with PlantFactory's installed
cherry bark and flower materials.

## Candidate family

- Two mature open-crown candidates derived from the editable English Oak graph.
- Two cascading candidates derived from the editable Crack Willow graph.
- Two young airy candidates derived from the editable Acer saccharum graph.

These graphs are structural starting points, not botanical identity claims. The
proof must expose the trunk, primary branch rhythm, crown silhouette, flower-card
quality, and human scale so a non-cherry-looking result can be rejected before
composition work begins.

## Approval artifact

Every candidate receives fixed front, three-quarter, silhouette, human-height,
bark/root, and canopy views. The contact sheet includes all six rows. Automated
evidence records source hashes, deterministic age/health/season/seed readback,
material replacements, geometry counts, dimensions, and runtime budgets.

The Blossom production scene remains untouched until Austen explicitly selects
the winning forms. Selection does not approve placement; the measured floor plan
and sightline study remain the next separate gate.

## Ownership

- Shared conditioning, optimization, rendering, contact-sheet, and verification
  owners: the existing PlantFactory bridge scripts under `scripts/`
- Evidence: `docs/superpowers/specs/blossom-plantfactory-family-r1/evidence/`

## Provenance

The source graphs and cherry materials are PlantFactory stock content installed
under `C:/ProgramData/e-onsoftware/PlantFactory`. Every input is pinned by byte
count and SHA-256. The project keeps a no-standalone-redistribution policy even
though the resulting models are intended only as embedded application assets.
