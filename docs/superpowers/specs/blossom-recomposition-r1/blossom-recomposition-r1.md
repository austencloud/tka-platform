## Composition thesis

Blossom becomes an asymmetrical twilight performance garden built around _ma_:
the empty interval between the performer deck, two mature crowns, water, and the
rear threshold. The scene uses two major trees, not a grove. The audience reads
performers first, the hero crown second, and the river, bridge, and torii as a
slow reveal during orbit.

## Placement rationale

- Crown A sits left-middle at `(-7.2, 9.4)` and supplies the hero silhouette.
- Crown B sits farther back and right at `(7.6, 13.6)`, so it balances A without
  creating a pink wall behind the performers. These viewer-fit positions keep
  both trunks visible at the performer camera while their crowns frame the sky.
- The river crosses the midground as a narrow S-ribbon. Its bridge stays left of
  the protected sightline.
- The torii spans 11 m at `(0, 24.5)`. Its posts remain outside the protected
  corridor while the lintel establishes a readable destination beyond the
  river instead of appearing as cropped posts at the edge of the default frame.
- Planting islands occupy the perimeter. Their job is to soften the garden edge,
  not fill every open patch.
