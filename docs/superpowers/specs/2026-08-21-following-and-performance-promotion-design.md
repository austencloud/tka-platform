# Following and Performance Promotion

**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Status:** Deferred decision specification  
**Parent:** `2026-08-21-browse-explore-you-public-contributions-design.md`

## Following decision

Creator following and collection following remain distinct. A followed
collection is a subscription to one authored collection and appears under You.

Before building a cross-type creator feed, compare three product models with
production evidence:

1. Creator roster with links to public portfolios.
2. Per-type `Following` filters within Sequences, Collections, or Visuals.
3. Balanced mixed publication feed.

No mixed activity projection is approved by this document. If the feed wins,
its own specification must own pagination, deduplication, per-type balancing,
retry safety, removal, privacy, empty states, and ranking. Client-side merging
of unbounded Firestore queries is forbidden.

Media events may not appear in any Following surface before the performance
archive independently earns promotion and the canonical media-presentation
policy is amended.

## Performance archive decision

Watch remains retired. Contextual performance and realization media may appear
on approved subjects and creator portfolios after the media-authority gates
pass. A performance-first Explore destination remains absent unless every
accepted Watch-retirement gate passes:

- three useful archive sections with six unique works each;
- eight participating creators;
- no creator above 40 percent of the visible catalog;
- eight new performances from four creators in the trailing 90 days;
- complete thumbnail, creator, typed-subject or approved-editorial identity,
  duration, projection, and consent metadata;
- enforced reporting, moderation, takedown, retention, caption, and revocable
  delivery policies.

If the gates fail, no disabled or supply-poor destination appears. If they pass,
the archive is editorial, not an infinite mixed feed, and the canonical
capability rule is deliberately updated to permit the reviewed presentation.

Promotion requires a checked-in production gate report plus anonymous-read,
moderation, reporting, consent-withdrawal, delivery-revocation, accessibility,
and full-viewport runtime proof.
