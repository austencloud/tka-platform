# Earth Compression Concept Study

Status: proposal only. Not approved. Does not replace the Root Chasm or alter the live museum.

This study explains the alternate Earth-room premise called **The Weight: Compression**. It uses the existing 34 × 24 metre Earth footprint, Fire's west entry, Air's south-east exit, and the three G/H/I performers.

## Spatial idea

The visitor crosses an alternating series of massive stone strata instead of circling a canyon. Each stratum contains one close performer habitat. The route ends at a registration point where sightlines through the three fractures place G, H, and I in one frame. The final walk to Air passes beneath the largest overhead mass.

## Visual deliverables

- `earth-compression-floor-plan.svg`: deterministic top-down plan using the real footprint and door program.
- `earth-compression-section.svg`: explanatory section showing human scale, performer cavities, overhead mass, and the release into Air.
- Raster concept studies are generated from these two diagrams as spatial references.

## Guardrails

- Exactly three performers: G, H, and I.
- All three must share a readable sightline at the registration point.
- The floor stays stable and walkable.
- No canyon, pit, rim circuit, garden, sci-fi machinery, or moving collision geometry.
- The green Fire transition may survive only as a narrow seam at the threshold.
- Major rock movement is implied through sound, dust, and tiny visual-only settling.

## Evaluation gate

The concept only advances if the plan reads without narration and the perspective study shows all three performers clearly. The current Root Chasm remains the Earth-room implementation until Austen explicitly approves a replacement direction.
