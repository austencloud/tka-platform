# Shape Engine Ratio Guide

## Outcome

Give the Kinetic Shape Engine a concise attribution surface and a permanent
public reference for translating between Lorq Nichols’ original Shape Matrix
labels, the engine’s prop-first ratios, and TKA turn values.

## Evidence boundary

- Lorq Nichols’ publication labels its three source families `1:1`, `1:3`, and
  `1:5`, with four even-petaled driving styles in each family.
- The Kinetic Shape Engine’s canonical domain functions display ratios as
  `prop rotations : hand cycles`. Those three families therefore read `1:1`,
  `3:1`, and `5:1` in the app.
- The TKA level system introduces zero turns at Level 1, whole turns at Level 2,
  halves and Float at Level 3, and quarter turns at Level 4. Shape Engine also
  exposes its negative-quarter `1:2` flower in the Level 4 band.
- The History archive already owns the sourced VTG and Lorq Nichols records.
  This feature links to those records instead of copying their source ledger.

## Owners and composition

- Reuse `BaseModal` with its `xl` size for the About dialog.
- Reuse `GuideShell`, `GuideSeo`, and the existing Guide navigation.
- Derive every TKA row from `matrixTurnsForLevel`, `levelForTurnValue`,
  `ratioLabel`, and `flowerPetals`. No second ratio table owns the math.
- Keep Lorq’s three source-family labels as sourced editorial data. They are
  not generated by TKA domain functions because they use Lorq’s reciprocal
  label convention.

## Copy and layout

The About dialog uses two readable columns on wide screens: Lorq’s foundation
and Austen Cloud’s independent work. It collapses to one column when the modal
cannot give both columns a useful reading measure. Detailed ratio instruction
moves to `/guide/ratios`.

The ratio guide answers the convention difference first, then shows Lorq’s
twelve driving styles, the complete Shape Engine Level 1–4 turn ladder, the
forward and inverse formulas, and direct links to Shape Engine and the two
relevant History records.
