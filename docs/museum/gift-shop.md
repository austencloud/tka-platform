# The Gift Shop

> This document describes the museum gift shop, accessible only after choosing YES in the Futures Chamber.

---

## Overview

Every museum has a gift shop. This one is no different - except some items are digital unlocks, some are fake in-game purchases, and some are links to actual products you can buy.

The blend of fake museum gift shop energy with real commerce is part of the joke.

---

## The Found Money Mechanic

Near the gift shop entrance, the player finds a crumpled $20 bill on the floor.

This is their spending money for fake items. It reinforces the gift shop illusion without requiring any real payment.

**Visual:** A slightly dirty $20 bill, folded, sitting near a trash can. Player picks it up automatically or by interaction.

### Not Enough Money

If the player tries to buy more than $20 worth of items, the cashier (a mannequin with a pre-recorded message) says:

> "I'm sorry, you don't have enough for that. Perhaps put something back?"

This reinforces the illusion without frustrating players - they still get to buy several items.

## Digital Unlocks (For TKA Scribe App)

These items are "free" (already earned by completing the museum) but presented as gift shop acquisitions. They unlock features in the actual TKA Scribe web app.

### Historical Style Generator

**Description:** Generate your word/sequence in any historical visual style.

| Style | Visual Treatment |
|-------|------------------|
| Paleolithic | Cave painting aesthetic - ochre, charcoal, primitive marks |
| Egyptian | Hieroglyphic style - formal, gold and blue accents |
| Renaissance | Codex page - handwritten marginalia, ink sketches |
| Victorian | Patent document - technical drawings, formal typography |
| Windows 93 | Retro pixel aesthetic - CRT scanlines, system fonts |

**How it works:** In TKA Scribe, when generating a sequence image, players can select these styles as output options.

### Scribe Certificate

**Description:** Your personalized certificate of Scribe membership, in your chosen era style.

- Downloadable as high-res image
- Printable
- Includes player's name/username
- Dated with museum completion date

### Spiral Badge

**Description:** A spiral icon appears next to your username in TKA Scribe.

- Subtle visual distinction
- Visible to other users
- Indicates museum completion

### Lore Codex

**Description:** Access to the full museum lore text from within TKA Scribe.

- All plaque text
- Historical timeline
- Order/Scribe information
- Readable anytime for reference

## Gift Shop Environment

### Visual Design

- Bright, commercial lighting (contrast to moody museum)
- Display racks and shelves
- Rotating display cases for "premium" items
- Checkout counter with mannequin cashier
- Background muzak (generic, slightly annoying)
- "Thank you for visiting!" signs

### The Cashier

A mannequin in a museum uniform stands behind the counter. Pre-recorded audio plays for various interactions:

| Trigger | Audio |
|---------|-------|
| Entering shop | "Welcome to the Museum Gift Shop. Take your time." |
| Purchasing item | "Excellent choice. The Scribes thank you for your support." |
| Not enough money | "I'm sorry, you don't have enough for that." |
| Exiting shop | "Thank you for visiting the Museum of Kinetic History." |

The mannequin doesn't move. This is slightly unsettling and part of the charm.

### Exit

The gift shop leads to the final exit. A door marked "EXIT" with a sign:

> Thank you for visiting.
>
> Remember what you learned.
>
> The future is in your hands.
>
> ─────
>
> [LINK TO TKA SCRIBE]

Clicking the exit opens the TKA Scribe web app (either in-game WebView or external browser).

## Design Notes

### The Tone

Gift shops are inherently absurd. Overpriced, full of junk nobody needs, positioned to extract maximum cash from visitors who just had an emotional experience. Leaning into this is funny.

But ALSO: some of this stuff is genuinely charming. A plush stone tablet that squeaks? A snow globe of Austen receiving divine knowledge? People might actually want these.

The line between ironic and sincere should be blurry.

### Future Expansion

The gift shop could expand with:
- Seasonal items ("Holiday Spiral Ornament")
- Limited editions tied to real-world events
- User-submitted designs (community merch)
- Collaboration items with real flow artists

For MVP: keep it simple. ~15 fake items, certificate, historical styles, badge, and links to existing real products.
