## Key Takeaways

### Vertical Slice Definition
A small, self-contained, fully playable section that shows the final experience. Unlike prototypes, it includes near-final gameplay, art, sound, and systems working together. Aim for 5-10 minutes of focused play.

### For Narrative Games Specifically
"If you exclude the narrative entirely, you sever the emotional anchor." Give enough story to establish who the player is, what they're doing, and why it matters. Don't need the whole script, just a taste.

### Build Order
1. Prototype (gray-box rooms, basic interaction)
2. Vertical slice (one room, fully finished, 5-10 min)
3. Full production (expand from proven slice)

### AI-Assisted UE5 Development (2026 State of Art)
- UE 5.7 has native in-editor AI assistant
- Blueprint visual scripting can be guided by AI via screenshot sharing
- Procedural Content Generation framework is production-ready in 5.7
- AI-assisted workflows are entering production pipelines

### Our Specific Advantage
Lore-first development means every room we build already knows: whose voice speaks, what era it represents, what word count the plaques use, what K hasn't annotated yet, what the player does. The container exists. Each room is an instance of a pattern, not a design exercise from scratch.
