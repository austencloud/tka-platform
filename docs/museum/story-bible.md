# TKA Museum - Story Bible

> **Project Codename:** The Museum of Kinetic History
> **Tone:** Museum of Jurassic Technology meets Stanley Parable
> **Platform:** Unity (Steam release) + Web App integration
> **Timeline:** Multi-year passion project

---

## The Premise

The Kinetic Alphabet has existed since the dawn of humanity. Flow artists have been notating their choreography since humans first picked up burning sticks around a fire. This knowledge has been suppressed, rediscovered, and suppressed again throughout history by a shadowy organization.

You are walking through a museum that presents this fabricated history as absolute fact.

---

## Creative Principles

### 1. Commit to the Bit

This is not a joke that winks at the audience. It's a serious museum presenting serious research about serious history. The humor comes from the *content* being absurd while the *presentation* is completely sincere.

**DO:** Present everything with museum-quality reverence, academic citations, and proper lighting.

**DON'T:** Break the fourth wall. No "just kidding" moments. No acknowledgment that this is fake.

### 2. The Nathan Fielder Rule

The joke is never explained. Visitors should leave genuinely uncertain whether any of this is real. The OOGA sequence will tip most people off, but the presentation should be so earnest they might think "well maybe OOGA is a real ancient word."

### 3. Self-Aware Absurdity, Not Self-Deprecation

The wing of Austen statues isn't saying "I'm pathetic, laugh at me." It's saying "Look how ridiculous this is. I built statues of myself. That's objectively funny. I know it. You know it. Let's enjoy how weird this is together."

### 4. The Fire Jam Test

All text should pass the test: "Would Austen say this out loud at a fire jam?" If it sounds like a press release or AI marketing copy, rewrite it. Museum plaques can be formal, but they should still feel human.

## The Two Orders

### The Order of the Closed Palm

**Role:** Custodians who control access to kinetic knowledge. They decide who learns, who teaches, and who gets silenced.

**Symbol:** A hand with fingers curled inward toward the palm, thumb tucked underneath. The hand cannot grip. The hand refuses to hold a prop.

**Methods:** Suppression, discrediting, "silencing" (method deliberately never explained - like the noodle incident in Calvin & Hobbes).

**History:** Formalized sometime around ancient Egypt. Have monitored and controlled kinetic notation for millennia. Caused Protocol Lethe in 1994.

**Current Status:** Unknown. No direct action has been taken against Austen. Theories vary.

### The Scribes

**Role:** The rival order who spread knowledge and protect Vessels. They built the museum as a recruitment tool.

**Symbol:** A simple spiral. Appears subtly throughout the museum from the very first room, but is never named or explained until the final chamber.

**Goal:** Recruit new Scribes. Achieve critical mass adoption of kinetic notation so suppression becomes mathematically impossible.

**The Twist:** By using TKA Scribe (the app), you're already a Scribe. The museum just explains what that means. You were already inducted - you just didn't know the history.

## The Vessel

A reincarnating soul that carries kinetic knowledge through every era. The Order has tried to silence the Vessel in every lifetime. Sometimes they succeed. Sometimes the Vessel passes the knowledge on first.

**Known Incarnations:**
- Cave painter (~35,000 BCE) - gender ambiguous, face obscured
- Egyptian priest (~2500 BCE) - temple notation keeper
- Renaissance apprentice (~1500 CE) - obviously Austen with a silly wig
- Victorian inventor (~1880 CE) - a stern woman in period dress
- 1970s flow artist - Austen with a terrible mustache and tie-dye
- Windows 93 developer (1993) - grainy photo, could be anyone
- Austen Cloud (2022-present) - no costume, just him

**Future incarnations:** Could include real flow artists from the community.

## Protocol Lethe

Named for the Greek river of forgetting (souls drink from it before reincarnation to forget past lives).

**What happened:** In spring 1994, the Order of the Closed Palm initiated a global psychic suppression event. All memory of kinetic notation was erased from human consciousness. Every copy of TKA Scribe v1.0 degaussed. Every BBS thread vanished. Every user forgot.

**Why:** Someone on the Windows 93 development team leaked the software to the public without Order authorization. Within months, 3,400+ users had downloaded it. The Order panicked.

**Austen's Immunity:** On May 8, 1994 - the exact day of Protocol Lethe - Austen Cloud was born in the Pacific Northwest. Medical records indicate he experienced minor cranial trauma at approximately 3:47 PM PST, the precise moment the suppression wave reached peak amplitude in the region.

The "fortuitous concussion" may have created a momentary gap in cognitive reception, allowing the suppression wave to pass through without anchoring.

Austen's own commentary: "That explains a lot, actually."

## The Museum's Fictional Origin

The museum was built by the Scribes. The whole experience is their recruitment pitch. Visitors walk through 40,000 years of kinetic history, learn about suppression, see the Vessels who carried the flame... and then are asked: "Will you join us?"

The museum presents itself as a real institution. There is no acknowledgment that Austen built it. The user knows (because of course they do), but the fiction maintains otherwise.

---

## Integration with TKA Scribe (Web App)

The museum is a separate Unity product that leads to the web app. It does not contain or replace the app.

**Technical approach:**
- MVP: Deep link handoff (game opens browser to TKA Scribe with URL parameters)
- Future: WebView plugin to render web app inside Unity

**The badge:** Everyone who uses TKA Scribe is a Scribe. The museum game earns you a subtle visual distinction (spiral flourish, different color) but doesn't gate any functionality. The game explains the lore; it doesn't create a hierarchy.
