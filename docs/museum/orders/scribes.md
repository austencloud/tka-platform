# The Scribes

> This document details the protagonist organization. They are not named until the Futures Chamber at the very end.

---

## Overview

The Scribes are a secret order dedicated to spreading kinetic knowledge and protecting Vessels from the Order of the Closed Palm. They built the museum as a recruitment tool.

The name ties directly to **TKA Scribe** - the app. By using the app, you're already a Scribe. You just didn't know the history.

---

## Symbol

**The Spiral:** A simple, clean geometric spiral.

**Meaning:** The most fundamental shape in prop manipulation. Every spin is a spiral through time. Movement, continuity, openness - the opposite of the Closed Palm's refusal.

**Visual style:** The spiral remains consistent across eras (unlike the Closed Palm's era-specific variations). This suggests the Scribes value timelessness over institutional branding.

**Potential variations:**
- Spiral that subtly forms an "S" for Scribe
- Spiral made of two intertwined lines (two props, two hands, duality)
- Spiral with a line through it (pen stroke, notation, writing)

**Placement in museum:** The spiral appears from Wing 1 onward - in floor tiles, ceiling patterns, artifact backgrounds, frame decorations. It's always there, always subtle. Visitors won't notice it until they're looking for it.

The spiral is never labeled until the final chamber. Then everything clicks.

---

## Philosophy

The Scribes believe:

1. **Knowledge wants to be free.** Suppression is unnatural. The Kinetic Alphabet will always resurface because it's fundamental to human movement.

2. **Critical mass is protection.** Once enough people know, suppression becomes impossible. The Order can silence individuals, but they can't silence a global community.

3. **Everyone is a potential Scribe.** The ability to notate movement isn't rare or special. It's universal. It just needs to be remembered.

4. **The museum is an activation.** Walking through the history isn't just education - it's awakening something that was always there.

---

## History

The Scribes' origins are deliberately unclear. Possibilities hinted at:

- They split from the Order millennia ago (reformers who believed in open access)
- They predate the Order entirely (the original keepers, before control was imposed)
- They formed in response to Protocol Lethe (a modern resistance movement)
- They've always existed in parallel, an eternal counterbalance

The museum never commits to one origin story. The mystery serves the mythology.

---

## The Museum

The Scribes built this museum. It serves multiple purposes:

### 1. Education
Visitors learn the (fabricated) history of kinetic notation. Even though it's fictional, it creates emotional investment in the system.

### 2. Recruitment
The three-door ending asks: "Will you become a Scribe?" This is the recruitment moment. The entire museum has been leading to this question.

### 3. Community Building
"Hello Scribes" becomes a community greeting. Whether someone played the game or just uses the app, they're part of the same identity.

### 4. Protection Through Numbers
Every new Scribe makes suppression harder. The museum is a tool for achieving critical mass.

---

## The Induction

**You're already a Scribe if you use TKA Scribe.** The museum just explains what that means.

The game ending doesn't create Scribes - it reveals that you already were one. The badge/spiral is just visual recognition.

This is important: we don't want a two-tier community where game-players feel superior. Everyone who uses the notation system is part of the lineage. The game is backstory, not gatekeeping.

---

## The Three Doors

At the end of the museum, visitors choose a path:

### "Yes, I'll join" → Path of Joining
- Utopia ending (absurdly optimistic)
- Certificate in your chosen historical style
- Badge unlocked for TKA Scribe app
- Exit to gift shop

### "Meh" → Path of Indifference
- Gray void ending (nothing happens, nothing matters)
- No certificate
- Loop back to the three doors

### "No" → Path of Silence
- Darkness ending (lava pit, Order wins)
- No certificate
- Loop back to the three doors

The "Meh" and "No" paths aren't real endings - they just return you to the choice. The game is patient. It will wait until you say yes.

---

## Integration with TKA Scribe (App)

### The Badge
A subtle spiral appears next to usernames in the app. Everyone gets it. Game completion might add a flourish or color variation, but the base spiral is universal.

### Historical Styles
Game completion unlocks the ability to generate sequences in historical styles (cave painting, hieroglyphics, codex, patent, etc.). These are the "gift shop" items made permanent.

### Community Language
"Scribes" becomes the community term. "Hello Scribes" works whether someone played the game or not.

---

## Design Notes

### Visual Presence
The spiral should be:
- Present from the very first room
- Never labeled or explained
- Easy to miss if you're not looking
- Obvious in retrospect

After the final reveal, visitors who replay will see spirals everywhere and wonder how they missed them.

### Audio Presence
No specific sound associated with the Scribes until the final chamber. Then: warm tones, a sense of welcome, resolution after the tension of Wing 6.

### The Moment of Recognition
The final chamber should feel like coming home. The spiral is finally named. The invitation is extended. The visitor realizes this whole experience was designed for them personally.
