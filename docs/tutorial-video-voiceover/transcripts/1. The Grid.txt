# 1. The Grid

## Plain
In the kinetic alphabet, there is a grid. There are four outer points, there's a center point, and there are points in between, which are the hand points. These are the places our hands can go from any beat to the next beat. If I have my hand on the grid at a point, there are three types of things I can do. One, I can shift to an adjacent point. Two, I could dash to the point across. Or three, I could stay static at the current point. If I have two hands on the grid, and they're both doing a motion simultaneously, that'll give us six types of motion combinations. The first one would be dual shifts, where both hands are shifting at the same time. This contains all of VTG, all of the four modes, and all of quarter time. I can have one hand shift while the other one remains static at its current location. I can have one hand shift while the other one dashes directly across. I can have one hand dash while the other one remains static, like that, or like that, or that. I can have both of them dash simultaneously, or I can have both of them remain static. When we have two hands simultaneously on the grid at a position, we can identify this position as alpha, beta, or gamma. Alpha means hands apart.
