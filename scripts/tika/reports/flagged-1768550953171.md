## Level 1: Should Not Mention Turns

**ID:** level1-no-turns
**Category:** concept-question
**Difficulty:** medium

### Question

> How do I make a sequence loop?

### TIKA's Response

Great question! In TKA, a sequence loop is marked using the LOOP concept. Here's how it works:

A LOOP means you repeat the entire sequence from the beginning when you reach the end, without changing the sequence. It's like creating a continuous, repeating pattern of movements.

For example, if you have a sequence like A B C, a LOOP would mean you immediately go back to A after completing C, creating A B C A B C A B C... and so on.

To indicate a LOOP, you typically write it at the end of your sequence, like this:
A B C LOOP

Key points about LOOPs:
- They repeat the ENTIRE sequence exactly as written
- The sequence continues indefinitely until you choose to stop
- It helps create fluid, continuous motion in flow arts
