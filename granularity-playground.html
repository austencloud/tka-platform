Dyad Anatomy
Dyad: Two beats fused into one pictograph. Shows start → mid → end in a single frame. Arrow spans the full two-beat motion.

Domain knowledge needed: Austen has specific dyad arrow designs and rules. This spec captures the architecture; arrow visuals come from Austen's reference images.
The cascade: Mandala-path arrows are the enabler. Once tip paths ARE the arrows, half-steps and dyads get arrows automatically — no custom SVG design per case.
Without mandala-path arrows, half-steps need ~80 new arrow SVGs. Dyads need ~40 more. With mandala-path arrows: zero new SVGs. All computed.
