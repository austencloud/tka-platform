The prop contract (why the 8-props ambush ends)

Today

Open 3D → restore everything from last time: 8 performers, 8 different props, 8 effects — even though you set the app to fans an hour ago.

Surprise every time the contexts diverge.

Plain open (new)

Structure restores — performer count, formation, camera, effects. Every prop re-seeds from your current app prop.

The stage always matches the app you're holding.

Preset open (new)

Loading a saved preset applies its snapshot verbatim — mixed props, effects, camera, scene, all of it. That's what presets are for.

Elaborate setups live in presets, not in ambush.

Where this lives (and where it doesn't)

Sequence viewer 3D — this design

One sequence, many forms. Every performer renders the sequence you're viewing; per-performer sequences are structurally impossible here (performer settings carry prop, effort, and effect — there is no sequence field to diverge), and they stay impossible.

The intro flow and Presets panel ship here first.

Stage module — already built for the rest

The "different performers, different sequences" experience exists today: the Stage module gives each performer their own sequence-clip lanes on a timeline, plus performer count, a formation editor, and its own scene picker.

Its future guided setup reuses these same cards — nothing here forks.
