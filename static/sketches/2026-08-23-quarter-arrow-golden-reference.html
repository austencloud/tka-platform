Quarter-Turn Arrow Golden Reference

The 32 generated *_0.25.svg arrows were rejected on 2026-08-23. This board defines what "correct" means before any regeneration: the visual contract expressed by the hand-authored legacy corpus (70 assets, measured by scripts/quarter-arrows/measure-legacy.mjs), the exemplars that carry it, and the exact grammar break in the rejected art. Approving this board approves the visual language, not any individual quarter-turn case.

1 · The visual contract (what every generated arrow must express)

C1 — Filled silhouette, never strokes

One closed filled path. The arrow's varying width is drawn into the outline itself.

Measured: 70/70 legacy assets fill-based, 0 stroke-based. Rejected art: 32/32 stroke-based.

C2 — Tapered organic body

The shaft is not constant-width: a slim tail, a gently swelling body, tapering into the head. Hand-drawn calligraphic weight, not a geometric polyline.

pro_0.5 (cleanest fit): tail 19.2 → shaft 19.3 → head barb 82.4 viewBox units.

C3 — Barbed arrowhead grown from the fill

The head is two swept barbs that flow out of the shaft outline — not a detached V or triangle cap. Barb span roughly 3–5.5× shaft width (median ≈4.3 on clean fits).

pro_0.5 head/shaft 4.26 · dash_1.0 4.99 · dash_1.5 4.04.

C4 — Arc family continuity

Turns values ornament one arc grammar. A 0.25 arrow must read as the sibling between its 0.0 and 0.5 neighbors — same curvature language, same head, slightly different sweep/ornament.

Legacy sweeps: pro 0.0 ≈144°, pro 0.5 ≈129°, dash 1.0 ≈96° (fitted circle, rmse/R ≤ 0.16).

C5 — Weight at pictograph scale

Arrows are read at grid-cell size next to props, dots, and the turn tuple. Shaft weight must match the legacy corpus at render scale so quarter arrows don't read lighter or heavier than their neighbors.

C6 — Canonical anchor and bounds

Each asset ships a deliberate anchor and tight bounds the placement tiers build on. The current quarter art's {0, 0} anchor fallback is a placeholder, not a convention.

C7 — Clearance envelope

The silhouette plus its placement must clear grid dots, both props, the other hand's arrow, and the TKA turn tuple at defined tolerances. Enforced by collision oracles in the review lab, not by eye.

5 · What approving this board means

  • The seven contract properties (C1–C7) define "visually correct" for all regenerated quarter arrows.
  • The parametric generator in @tka/render-core will be fitted to this grammar; the 32 current SVGs are discarded scaffolding.
  • Quarter arrows must sit between their 0.0 and 0.5 neighbors in the arc family (C4), per motion type and orientation family.
  • Placement calibration happens after art, through the existing tier system, with collision oracles — not hand-nudging.
  • You will review one canonical representative per proven equivalence class (32) plus machine-detected outliers — not all 112 states.
