Sun Chamber — “The Sundial”

Your bearing from the centre is the sun's azimuth and it sits at your back; your distance from the centre is its elevation. Walking inward walks you to noon. The spiral crossing sweeps exactly 90°, so the move that carries you to zenith performs the room's own phase offset. You leave by standing in the eye and letting the ground lift you through the ceiling onto the Moon.
