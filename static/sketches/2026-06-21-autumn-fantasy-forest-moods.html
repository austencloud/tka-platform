★ Recommended · Fusion

Enchanted Autumn Dusk

One mood that absorbs the best of all four. Autumn’s skeleton (crimson-gold canopy, shimmering falling leaves) under a golden-hour-to-dusk sky, with bioluminescent magic layered in — glowing mushroom groves, will-o-wisps drifting between ancient mossy trunks, a still pond mirroring the last light. The performer’s motion pulses the wisps and mushroom glow, the way touching the Ocean jellyfish chimes.

Why this one: keeps the “Autumn” identity (the name still means something), maximally separates from green Forest + snowy Winter, and lands on the night-glow palette where our emissive halation + particle + interaction tech already shines. It’s a single coherent scene — not four — so it’s one Blender pass, not four. The warm/cool split (low sun vs. waking glow) gives the orbit camera something different to reveal from every angle.
