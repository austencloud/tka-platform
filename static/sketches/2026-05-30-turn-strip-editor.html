Pattern Strip Editor — one engine, three drawers

Every drawer is the same primitive: Period (length) × Rhythm (which beats are active) × Value on active beats → an editable strip. The strip is the source of truth — editing is primary. Chips are shortcuts that stamp values in, and they auto-highlight when the strip happens to match them. Turns & reversals share the per-hand rhythm catalog; duration reuses the same engine, single-lane.
