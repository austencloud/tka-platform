The experience, in order

  1. 1Approach. Descending S-curve from the Squeeze. Water heard before it is seen; walls turn wet-specular; first drips.
  2. 2The sump. The path walks down under the waterline. ~10 m fully submerged: absorption fog, caustics, bubbles, muffled audio, fish overhead. A gentle bend hides the grotto until —
  3. 3Surfacing. Steps rise out of the water and the grotto opens all at once. This is the reveal frame.
  4. 4Causeway. Raised stone path (+1.2 m) with three half-round overlooks, one aligned to each alcove. Carved balustrade at the pool edge.
  5. 5Waterfall. ~10.5 m plunge from a sunlit crack, god rays and a faint rainbow in the mist. The room's light source and voice.
  6. 6Mirror pool + glowworm ceiling. Black water doubles everything; a bioluminescent constellation hangs above it.
  7. 7Habitat alcoves. Three cavemen in procession at the waterline — A (pro/pro), B (anti/anti), C (anti/pro) — each in a pool of firelight with a glowing painted pictograph behind, doubled in reflection. Rock fins keep each a solo encounter.
  8. 8Gate. The causeway's north leg dead-ends at a museum gate beside alcove C — the close view, through bars.
  9. 9Exit. Ascending bend toward Fire; the water sounds fade behind.

Design commitments

  1. Cave-native water only. No reef, no coral, no tropical species mix. Waterfall, sump, mirror pool, drip rain, mist, glowworms — all things caves actually do.
  2. The underwater walk survives as the entrance: every visitor passes through the sump to get in (the exit to Fire is a separate dry passage). Absorption + distortion + caustics from the ocean scene run only while below the waterline.
  3. Existing fish, existing boids. The FishBoids system and current GLB catalog as-is; species chosen for dark-water reading. No new fish assets.
  4. Four spatial layers per the rebuild handoff: approach/occlusion → public apron (causeway + overlooks) → gate (balustrade + barred gate + the pool itself) → recessed habitat (alcoves).
  5. Pedagogy by comparison. A, B, C share one hand path; the procession shows prop rotation as the only variable. Pictographs painted as glowing cave art — pre-alphabetic, no Latin letters on rock.
  6. One caveman per letter — three distinct automatons, per the 19-base-sequence roster (founding collection "TKA 1: Learning Letters").
