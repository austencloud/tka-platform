2026-08-03 · Under the water, out of the water, around the water. The middle of the route becomes a real flooded world: ~30 m walked on the bottom with rock overhead and no sign of air, cave-native life thickening mid-passage, then a stair that breaks the surface straight into the doubled firelight. The grotto is a ring: arrive center-south, circle the mirror pool, pass A → B → C at close range across a 4 m channel, exit east to Fire. North is to the RIGHT.

The beats

  1. The descent — approach ramp, waterline glow ahead, water to the knees, then the descent stair: the ceiling drops with you.
  2. The door of water — the surface closes overhead. Rock roof from here on; the only light is ahead.
  3. The bloom — mid-passage, the dark gallery opens into its most alive pocket: waving plants, pale cave fish crossing the path. The "no air" low point is also the wonder peak.
  4. The turn — last bend; a warm glow bleeds down through water from the surfacing stair. Direction is never in doubt.
  5. Surface break — mid-stair landing at −2.3: the head clears water and the FIRST air is the money shot — black pool ahead doubling three firelights, symmetric, framed by the stair walls.
  6. The procession — around the pool and along the walkway: A, then B, then C at 5–6 m across the channel, each fully legible, still unreachable across water. Waterfall terminates the channel vista west.
  7. The threshold — a carved arch with gold bars frames the exit leg; Fire's warm glow pulls you out east.
