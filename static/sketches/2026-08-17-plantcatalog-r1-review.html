From Quercus robur forest HD, keeping all 105,564 foliage triangles and all seven epiphyte species. This is the tree you stand next to.

Rejected on the way kept as evidence

Every triangle count was green when this was produced. Only the frame caught it. Blender's COLLAPSE decimate takes a ratio and nothing else, so it reaches that ratio whatever it costs the shape: the twig tubes' cross-section rings collapsed into flat ribbons, and a ribbon spanning two distant points is a large triangle that catches the sun. A per-material triangle budget then deleted the LD trunk outright. Reduction moved to an error-bounded simplifier, which is what the Forest environment bake already uses on its trees.
